# KataTalk Terms of Service (Draft)

**Status:** This draft is not final customer terms or legal advice.

## Service

KataTalk currently provides KataGo-derived numeric, move, PV, and deterministic
review output for user-uploaded Go-game SGF files. The repository's generative
natural-language adapter is not connected to the released product path.
Analysis may be delayed, unavailable, incomplete, or incorrect. Users remain
responsible for reviewing output.

If generative commentary is enabled, it must be labelled as AI-generated,
grounded in cited analysis evidence, and accompanied by a deterministic
fallback. It is educational post-game review, not a guarantee of the best move
or professional advice, and must not be used for assistance during a live game.

## Accounts and Content

Users must protect their account credentials and upload only content they are entitled to process. Users must not bypass authentication, payment, rate limits, job ownership checks, or service security controls.

## Credits and Payments

The current product path uses Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout for one-time credit packs, not subscriptions. Credits are granted from verified payment webhooks and recorded in a server-side ledger. A checkout success redirect alone does not grant credits.
